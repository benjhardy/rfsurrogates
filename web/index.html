Filter Surrogate

Edge-coupled band-pass filter, 3–7 GHz, on RO4350B. A coupled-line model plus a network trained on 261 Palace solves. Simulated only.

Definitions

f0: centre of |S21|². BW: widest band within 3 dB of the peak; "split" when the passband has two peaks. Return loss: worst |S11| between the outer reflection zeros. Spread: seed-to-seed deviation of |S21| in the passband.

How good is the surrogate?

Error on 26 Palace designs that no model saw in training.

Limits

BW error uses the 6 test designs with one passband.

Mesh. The training solves use a coarse mesh. On 10 designs solved again with a finer mesh, the coarse mesh is 1.03 dB off and the model is 0.91 dB off.

Prior. With 10 training solves, the prior gives 1.8 dB against 9.5 dB without it. From about 100 solves, the model without the prior is better.

Spread. The band is smaller than the true error. Use it as a lower bound.
